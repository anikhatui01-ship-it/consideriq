## 1. Overview
ConsiderIQ provides software for analyzing AI-mediated buyer journeys and brand visibility. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, how it may be shared, retention, security, and user choices.

## 2. Information we collect
Depending on use of the service:
- account email/name and authentication information
- organization/company information
- brand/project information
- public website URLs and retrieved public page content
- competitors, buyer personas, prompts and scenarios
- AI simulation conversations and responses
- citations/source metadata returned by providers
- usage, technical and security logs
- waitlist information

## 3. How we use information
We may use information to:
- provide and secure the service
- authenticate users
- run simulations
- analyze requested public websites
- store project history
- provide support
- prevent abuse and fraud
- troubleshoot and improve the service where permitted
- send service communications
- send marketing communications where legally permitted and consent is required

## 4. AI processing
ConsiderIQ may send user-provided prompts, project information, and content derived from public websites to AI providers configured in the production service.
